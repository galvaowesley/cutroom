How does a model learn? It rolls downhill. Start with one weight.

Zoom into any point, and the curve turns into a line. Its slope is the gradient.

Step against the slope. The new weight is the old weight, minus the learning rate times the slope. The flatter the curve, the smaller the step.

Now, change the learning rate. Too small, and it crawls. Just right, and it settles. Too big, and it overshoots.

Add a second weight, and the curve becomes a surface. Now there are two slopes, one per weight. Together, they are the gradient. Step against it: the same rule works in any dimension.

Billions of weights. One rule.
