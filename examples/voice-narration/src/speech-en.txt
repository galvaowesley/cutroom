cutroom. Media for coding agents, made on your machine.

It works in Claude Code, Codex, Antigravity, Gemini and Kiro.

Decks, diagrams, figures, math videos, motion, voice, graphics and edits. Fourteen skills, one folder per piece.

Bring your brand, or build one. Your voice too: with consent, it clones locally.

Nothing leaves the machine, and every version is pinned. Agents can even work in parallel.

Start with install dot py. Then, cutroom init.
