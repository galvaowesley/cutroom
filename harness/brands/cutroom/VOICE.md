# cutroom voice

> How pieces in this brand write and speak. Read before any script, slide title or narration.

- **Address:** speak to the audience as "you". Plain second person, never "the user" or "one".
- **Tone:** bold, but never loud for nothing. Playful, but never silly. Technical, but never jargon for its own sake.
  The color shouts; the words stay precise.
- **Numbers:** in the middle of the sentence and compared with a reference ("85.5%, against 0.4% for exact
  matching"). The number carries the weight, so adjectives don't have to.
- **Avoid:** superlatives (very, huge, incredible, best, revolutionary), alarm words (critical, alarming, drastic),
  corporate filler (leverage, synergy, seamless), exclamation marks. Emoji only in social posts, one at most.
- **Sentences:** short, one idea each. In narration, punctuation is the pause.
- **Name:** "cutroom", always lowercase, also at the start of a sentence.
- **Language:** English by default; any language the request asks for.
