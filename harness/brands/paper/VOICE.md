# Paper voice

- **Address:** "you" in explainers; "we" for the authors in reports.
- **Tone:** measured, but never dry. Precise, but never pedantic. Curious, but never speculative.
- **Numbers:** with their reference and their uncertainty ("12% to 15%, 95% CI of the difference: 1 to 5 points").
- **Avoid:** superlatives, alarm words, hype, emoji.
- **Sentences:** complete and unhurried; one claim per sentence.
- **Name:** "Paper" in running text; the logo is lowercase.
- **Language:** English by default.
